Toy fences instead of walls

Concept renders, not the game. The 8 homes stay in the curved row with open fronts. The walls between them are gone; each home gets its own toy fence down both sides, and buying a level swaps the fence, the floor and the props in a short build-up. Comment on any photo by its number.

Pick one

  1. A · a new toy fence every level (photos 1, 4–13). Popsicle sticks, clothes pegs and socks, toy bricks, pencils, candy canes, glow sticks, gold coins. Matches the 7 home themes and the 7 Speed wheels you already have. My pick
  2. B · crayon fences for everyone (photo 2). One look; it gets taller and more colourful each level, neon at Lv 6, gold at Lv 7.
  3. C · toy-brick walls for everyone (photo 3). A low brick wall that gains a row of bricks each level.

Why A: like the video, every level is a new place, so a rich home reads from across the room without the sign, and each upgrade is something new to see.

Also tell me

  • Fence height. As drawn they're about 3 players tall at Lv 1 and 5 at Lv 7. You still see in from your camera, but a mouse can't see over. Keep, or lower?
  • The upgrade moment (photos 14–17, about 1.6 s): build it like this?
  • The back. One stud-and-board wall now runs behind all 8 homes, and each home's themed back wall sits in front of it. OK?
  • Nothing else changes: same homes, same pens, same open fronts. The plots get a bit wider at the back (they fill the whole wedge where the wall used to be).

The upgrade moment

From the video: one press, the whole plot changes, a ring sweeps the floor and LEVEL UP! fills the screen. What we add: the change is built in front of you instead of a hard cut, the corner post lands last, your toys cheer, and the banner says what you got.
