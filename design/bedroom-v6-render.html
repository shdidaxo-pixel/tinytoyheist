The bedroom as one long run

A long straight room like Steal an Egg, but at mouse scale: 420 wide and 1700 long, with walls 320 tall and a giant bed. All 8 homes sit side by side in a curved wall at the start, inside the wall framing with wall between each one. Their fronts are open, so everyone can see everyone's home and you can look up the studs to the top. A small safe strip runs in front of them. Then three bands, each with bigger toys and more noise: carpet, wood, creaky boards. The bed is along the right-hand wall and the hallway door is straight ahead. Night lighting follows Dylan's photo 2; "Lights on" follows photo 1. Drag to look around, or pick a view.
