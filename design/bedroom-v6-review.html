Tiny Toy Heist · Kid's Bedroom · concept v6

The bedroom as one long run

Concept renders, not the game. All 8 homes sit side by side in a curved wall at the start, and the room runs long and straight from there to the hallway door. Comment on any photo by its number.

What changed

  • All 8 homes in one curved row inside the wall, with solid wall between each home.
  • Home fronts are open (drywall torn away), so you see everyone's toys and can look up the studs, blocking and cables to the top.
  • Bigger homes, 130 wide and 170 deep, so the toys can roam: the back of each home is a toy pen, dressed for the level (cardboard, socks, bricks, wood, candy, neon, gold), and the toys wander it freely instead of standing on slots.
  • The Speed hamster wheel levels up with the home: bigger, faster and made of better stuff each level, up to a gold wheel at Level 7.
  • A teal safe zone in front of the homes, 140 deep: bigger than the first strip, still much smaller than a whole band.
  • A smaller room so the toys and homes read bigger: the run is 420 wide and 1700 long, with walls 320 tall. The bed and furniture are a bit smaller, and the sleeping toys under blankets are about 35% bigger.
  • Three bands down the run: carpet (Small toys), wood (Medium), creaky boards (Large), each clearly coloured and labelled.
  • The train track is a winding loop: it snakes down both sides of the run and cuts across it twice, near the start and by the bed. The train has 5 cars counting the engine.
  • With the lights on, the cat gets aggressive: it leaves its patrol and pounces at the nearest mouse (photo 16).
  • The hallway door is straight ahead at the end. The bed is along the right-hand wall.
  • Much less clutter: no sock heap, bookshelf, beanbag, rug or scattered props. Hazards stay: cat, mouse traps, loose boards, baby monitor, flashlight, the train.

Still to decide

  • Scale: does the room now feel the right size next to the toys and homes, or shrink it more?
  • The start area round the homes is wider than the run: it needs that width to fit 8 bigger homes in the curve. OK, or would you rather have a different shape?
  • Night look (photo 2 lighting) as the default, lights-on (photo 1) when the Kid wakes: still right?
