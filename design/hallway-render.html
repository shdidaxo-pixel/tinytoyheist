The Hallway

Straight on through the bedroom's far-wall door: a corridor 280 wide and 800 long, longer and narrower than the bedroom, on loud wood (noise x2) with a quiet red runner down the middle that is the cat's route. Small toys in the first half, Medium in the second, 8 in all, either side of the runner. Landmarks against the walls: shoe rack, umbrella stand and coats, key table, the cat's bed. Straight ahead the Kitchen door, on the right the Living Room door, both ajar behind baby gates. A baby gate in the bedroom door is the Strength 3 lock. Drag to look around, or pick a view.
