The Hallway

Concept renders, not the game. Nothing is built in Studio yet. You go through the bedroom's far-wall door and keep running straight: a longer, narrower room on loud wood, with a quiet red runner down the middle that the cat patrols. Comment on any photo by its number.

What's in it

  • Same house, new room: the bedroom wallpaper pattern recoloured plum, the same voxel wood tiles in a warmer honey oak.
  • A burgundy runner down the middle is quiet (x1) but it's the cat's route, so the quiet way is the dangerous way. The wood either side is x2.
  • Small toys in the first half, Medium after the yellow line, always off the runner, so grabbing one means stepping onto the loud wood.
  • Landmarks stay against the walls: shoe rack you can hide under, umbrella stand with a raincoat and scarf, key table with a toy underneath, the cat's bed and bowls. The middle stays clear.
  • Next rooms are teased: the Kitchen door straight ahead (Strength 6) and the Living Room door on the right (Strength 10) are ajar behind baby gates, lit from inside, with a glowing toy you can see. The attic hatch cord dangles overhead (Rebirth 1).
  • A baby gate in the bedroom doorway is the Strength 3 lock (photo 4). You can see in, and it swings open once you're strong enough.
