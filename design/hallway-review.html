Concept renders, not the game. Nothing is built in Studio yet. Round 2, with your answers folded in: you go through the bedroom's far-wall door and keep running straight into a longer, narrower, louder room with its own cat and a Roomba. Comment on any photo by its number.

What changed from your answers

  • Length stays 800. Speed upgrades will cover it.
  • No runner. Wood (noise x2) all the way, as in the plan.
  • A second cat. The hallway has its own smoky-grey cat (the bedroom's is orange). It walks the middle end to end and turns round at its bed by the Kitchen door (photo 8).
  • A Roomba. It loops the hallway sucking up any toy someone dropped and dumps it at its dock on the left wall. The pile there is a jackpot worth raiding (photo 9).
  • Baby gates stay as the locks: Strength 3 (bedroom door), 6 (Kitchen), 10 (Living Room). The Living Room door stays on the side wall.
  • Bedroom door swung flat against the hallway wall (photo 1), with a hiding nook behind it.
  • The Kid stays in the bedroom. When he wakes, the hallway gets busier and louder instead: lights on, the cat pounces, the Roomba goes on turbo (photo 15).
