Trip times

Measured along the drawn routes at Roblox's default walk speed (16 studs a second), coming back at that toy size's carry speed. The design target is a 30–120 second run.

Why it's laid out this way

  • All 8 mouse holes are in the Hallway. It's the hub between the two loot rooms, so every player passes everyone else's door and sign. Raids happen on the way to loot, not as a separate trip.
  • The Hallway runner is the quiet lane. A strip of carpet down the middle of a loud wood floor. Dragging on it is x1 noise; step off it with a Medium toy and you double your noise. It's also the cat's route later, so the safe lane stops being safe.
  • The Giant sleeps in the Bedroom, the best loot is in the Kitchen. Close loot is quiet but cheap; far loot is on loud tile and worth 2.5x. The Giant's snack run goes through the Hallway to the fridge, straight past every mouse hole.
  • Every room has two hiding spots within 8 seconds of any spawn. Getting caught should feel like a mistake, not bad luck.
  • Locked doors show what's next. The Living Room door and the Garage door are visible from the main routes, with a glowing toy behind each gap.
