The new bedroom: open plots and things that bite

The room keeps its curved shape so you can see every base, but it's much smaller: the curved wall moves in from radius 930 to 600 and the room is 840 wide instead of 1200. Bases are no longer hidden behind the wall. Each one is an open plot on the floor: a studded toy baseplate in the owner's colour, a low brick fence you can see over, an open gate facing the bed with a name sign, and the mouse hole behind it where you spawn. The floor now fights back too: a prowling cat, mouse traps, loose creaky boards, toy bricks, a baby monitor and the Kid's flashlight at 80+ noise. Drag to look around, or pick a view.

Every angle

8 open plots around the curve, all the same distance from the bed. Each is a studded baseplate in the owner's colour, about 75 to 95 wide and 135 deep.
Low brick fences (about chest height) with studs on top. You can see into every plot, but you go in and out through the gate.
Fence theme shows level: cardboard (L1), toy bricks (L2), candy (L3), gold (L4). Level 3+ adds a second deck on pillars with more slots.
Red bars across a gate mean it's locked. The sign over every gate shows the owner and money per second.
Mouse holes are in the wall right behind each plot. That's where you spawn.
Floor rings: carpet by the plots (x1 noise), wood in the middle (x2), creaky boards by the bed (x3).
The cat prowls the wood ring. You hear its bell first; get close and it pounces.
Mouse traps sit in the lanes between plots and in the corners. SNAP sends you home.
Loose boards on the creaky ring are lifted a little: step on one and it creaks loudly.
The baby monitor lights a green patch in front of the bed. Noise inside it counts double.
At 80+ noise the Kid sits up and sweeps a flashlight. Get caught carrying a toy and you drop it.
Hiding spots: under the bed, behind the beanbag, the boxes and the toy chest.
