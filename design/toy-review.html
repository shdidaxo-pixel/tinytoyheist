The voxel toy models, with their idle animations, rarity glows and mutations. These are drawn from the same data Studio builds the toys from, so what you see here is what's in the game. Comment on any photo by its number.

What's new

  • This batch: the whole Kid's Bedroom set (6 MVP toys plus the 8 new ones) as voxel models, with the effects and mutations below. The other rooms, the events and the specials are being built now and will appear on this page as they finish.
  • Each toy plays its catalog idle animation (waddle, hop, twirl, pop out…), and the bigger ones have their own little effect: steam, sparks, flames, bubbles.
  • Rarity glows you can read at a glance: nothing for Common, a floor ring from Uncommon up, sparkles from Rare, an aura and light for Legendary, Mythic and Secret.
  • Sleeping Epic and up toys leak their colour from under the blanket for a second every 8 seconds, and every wake-up bursts confetti and a shockwave in the rarity colour.
  • 10 mutations, 3 of them new. Each is one shared piece of code that works on any toy.

Mutations

About 1 toy in 8 wakes up mutated. The rarer the mutation, the more it pays.
